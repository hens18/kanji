How a visit works

Four steps. Then repeat step three.

  1. 1

    Grab a seat

    Booth or bar. Tell your server you are going all you can eat, or order à la carte.

  2. 2

    Graze the belt

    Light bites roll past on the conveyor. Take whatever looks good.

  3. 3

    Order anything

    Rolls, nigiri, hibachi, takoyaki. Ask your server each round, as much as you like.

  4. 4

    Watch it arrive

    Your order rides the shuttle rail, made fresh, straight to your table.
